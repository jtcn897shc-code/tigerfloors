Questions

Before we talk.

What areas do you cover?

Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Langley, and Mission — the communities I've lived and sold in for fifteen years.

What does it cost to work with you as a buyer?

For buyers, my commission is paid by the seller in almost all cases — my guidance costs you nothing out of pocket.

How do you decide a listing price?

Live comparables, current buyer demand, and an honest walk-through of your home — not a formula, and never a flattering number just to win the listing.

How fast do you respond?

Same day, usually within the hour. Text is fastest. Most deals are won and lost in response time, and I treat it that way.

Can I see homes on evenings or weekends?

Yes — most showings happen outside business hours, and I plan around your schedule, not mine.
